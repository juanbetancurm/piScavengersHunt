# 𝛑 Pi Day Challenge
### *Where Mathematics Meets Adventure!*

**An interactive treasure hunt that transforms Pi Day into an exciting campus-wide mathematical adventure.**

---

## 🎯 What is Pi Day Challenge?

Pi Day Challenge is an innovative web-based treasure hunt game designed specifically for Pi Day celebrations! This engaging educational experience transforms traditional mathematics learning into an exciting campus adventure where students become mathematical detectives, combining physical exploration with digital problem-solving.

## 🗺️ How the Adventure Works

### **Phase 1: Team Formation & Registration**
- 👥 Students form teams and register online
- 📝 Enter team details and member information
- 🎲 Each team receives a randomized puzzle sequence for fair play

### **Phase 2: The Hunt Begins**
- 🔍 **Follow Location Hints**: Teams receive clues directing them to specific campus locations
- 🏃‍♀️ **Physical Exploration**: Students search around campus for hidden codes
- 🔑 **Code Discovery**: Find and enter secret codes to unlock the next challenge

### **Phase 3: Mathematical Challenges**
- 🧮 **Pi-Related Puzzles**: Solve problems involving circle geometry, Pi formulas, and mathematical concepts
- ⚡ **Real-Time Scoring**: Earn points based on accuracy (5 points correct, 2 points for attempts)
- 📊 **Instant Feedback**: Receive immediate results and explanations

### **Phase 4: Competition & Results**
- 🏆 **Live Leaderboard**: Track team progress and rankings
- ⏱️ **Time-Based Scoring**: Rankings consider both accuracy and completion speed
- 🎉 **Celebration**: Final results and recognition for all participants

## 📚 Educational Objectives

**Mathematical Concepts:**
- Circle geometry and sector calculations
- Famous Pi formulas (Wallis Product, Gregory-Leibniz series)
- Volume and area calculations involving Pi
- Mathematical reasoning and problem-solving

**21st Century Skills:**
- **Teamwork & Collaboration**: Students work together to solve challenges
- **Digital Literacy**: Navigate web-based interfaces and digital tools
- **Critical Thinking**: Analyze clues and solve complex problems
- **Physical Activity**: Promotes movement and campus exploration

**Engagement Benefits:**
- Transforms abstract mathematics into tangible experiences
- Combines physical and mental challenges
- Builds excitement around mathematical concepts
- Encourages peer learning and cooperation

**Responsive Web Design:**

## 🎓 Grade-Level Adaptability

The game automatically adapts mathematical complexity based on student grade levels:
- **Basic Level**: Fundamental Pi concepts and simple calculations
- **Intermediate Level**: Applied geometry and formula applications  
- **Advanced Level**: Complex mathematical reasoning and multi-step problems

## 📱 Technology Stack

- **Backend**: Spring Boot, Java 17
- **Database**: MySQL with JPA/Hibernate
- **Frontend**: Thymeleaf, Responsive CSS, JavaScript
- **Deployment**: Railway/Cloud-ready configuration
- **API**: RESTful endpoints with Swagger documentation

## 🏁 Getting Started

### **For Educators:**
1. Deploy the application to your preferred cloud platform
2. Configure the MySQL database with your puzzle content
3. Set up campus locations and corresponding codes
4. Launch the game URL for student access

### **For Students:**
1. Form your team (2-4 members recommended)
2. Register at the game website
3. Follow location hints to find codes around campus
4. Solve Pi-related puzzles to earn points
5. Check the leaderboard to see your progress!
